02

Teaching Philosophy

My work with performers splits two ways: nurturing a flexible voice toward optimal coordination, and forging in each performer an interpretive instinct, an understanding of the human condition, and an awareness of what contributes to powerful stage presence.

—Safety first. Every new group starts with vulnerability and shame resilience, because singing and acting ask people to be seen.
—Motor learning. Isolate one coordination, train it under different conditions, then unite it back into the whole instrument.
—Tactical actioning. A transitive verb for every beat change, so the work shifts from feeling to pursuing.
—Economy of gesture and eye focus. Restraint clarifies subtext.
