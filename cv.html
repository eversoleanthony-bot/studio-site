Research interests
Tactical actioning; beat and tactics systems in dramatic coaching for singers; eye focus and physical movement in singing-actor training; vulnerability and risk-taking for the singing actor; chakra and movement for the singing actor; Gerald Finzi’s song settings of Thomas Hardy; melody sources of Puccini’s Orientalist operas; sexism, imperialism, and exploitation in operatic traditions; verismo and the Giovane Scuola; Russian opera of the 19th century.
