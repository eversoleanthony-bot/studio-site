Anthony Eversole joins vocal science, motor learning, and actor training in his work as a performer, teacher, researcher, and director of the singing actor.

About

Anthony Eversole is a baritone, voice pedagogue, and director with the Doctor of Musical Arts from the University of Maryland.

He has taught applied voice for more than fifteen years, in private studios, K-12 classrooms, and university voice, musical theatre, and opera programs. His work centers on evidence-based, motor learning-driven vocal technique, paired with tactical, action-driven acting.
