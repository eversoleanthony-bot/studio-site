Directing

Anthony Eversole directs opera, musical theatre, and concert programs for mainstage productions, schools, and professional companies. His productions have been staged for The Crane Opera Ensemble, Oregon State University, and the University of Oklahoma.

Approach

Direction begins with the text. Each beat of a scene or song is given an active, transitive verb, so performers pursue something instead of playing a general emotion. Eye focus and economy of gesture follow from those choices, and the physical and vocal coordination underneath is trained so it holds up under the pressure of performance.

Inquiries about directing, vocal direction, and clinics are welcome through the contact page.
