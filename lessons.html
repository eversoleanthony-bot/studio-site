Questions

Do you teach in person or online?

Both. In-person lessons are at Day Murray Music in Murray and at the West Valley studio, and online lessons are available anywhere over Zoom or FaceTime. We confirm the format when you schedule, and it can change from week to week.

Can you come to my home?

Yes, by request and depending on my schedule. Travel lessons include a surcharge, so reach out to check availability and rates.

How do bundles work?

Credits are tracked and used through the booking portal each time you book. Bundles expire so progress stays steady: 60 days for a 4-pack and 120 days for an 8-pack. Studio space is limited, so unused credits can't be extended or transferred.

What do I need for online lessons?

Good audio matters most. Before your first online lesson, I recommend this setup:

  • Wired headphones. Bluetooth adds a delay that makes real-time feedback unreliable.
  • An external microphone (USB or XLR). Built-in laptop and tablet mics compress and color your sound, which makes feedback harder to trust.
  • A wired internet connection (Ethernet) when you can. Wi-Fi can degrade the sound that matters most for pitch and tone.
  • Zoom's Original Sound / High-Fidelity Music Mode, with noise suppression, echo cancellation, and automatic gain control turned off.
  • A second device for accompaniment tracks. Playing tracks from your lesson device causes sync problems and can cut your voice out when you start singing.
Why a second device for accompaniment?

Playing tracks from your lesson device causes lag, and Zoom can treat the track as background noise and cut your microphone when you start singing. Playing tracks from a separate phone or tablet keeps everything clean and in sync. A wired speaker is the most reliable, and a Bluetooth speaker works well in most rooms.

Can I switch between in-person and online?

Yes. Book in person one week and online the next. Your package credits work for either.

How do you handle sheet music?

Everything is digital. You will get a shared folder with studio exercises, public domain scores, and your current repertoire. For copyrighted music, you may need to buy your own copy, digital or print, for personal use.

What if I need to reschedule?

That is fine. You can change your lesson time through your booking confirmation up to 24 hours ahead without losing your credit. Cancellations or no-shows inside 24 hours can't be refunded or rescheduled, because that time is held for you.
